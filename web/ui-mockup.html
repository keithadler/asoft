Applesoft BASIC for DOS — Turbo Vision layout

80×43 text mode (smFont8x8). 80×25 cannot hold a 40×24 Apple screen plus a frame, a menu bar and a status line, so the app switches to the 8×8 font at startup. That buys room for the two side panes as well.
