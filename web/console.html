Faithful bugs

PRINT .1+.2 says .3

Applesoft carried nine significant digits in its 5-byte floats, so the answer really did come out clean. Nothing is rounded for show.

PRINT 2^33 = 2^33 + 1 says 1

The 5-byte float's mantissa is 32 bits, so the +1 falls off the end and the two are equal — verified against the genuine ROM.

10 TOTAL=5 lists as TO TAL = 5

The tokenizer finds the keyword TO inside the variable name. Real Applesoft programmers learned to avoid such names.

ONERR leaks memory

Trap errors in a loop and the program dies with ?OUT OF MEMORY after exactly sixty of them. LOAD ONERRFIX.BAS shows the leak and the classic CALL -3288 workaround.

The screen is the Apple's

Forty columns, and eighty after PR#3 (PR#0 comes back). GR and HGR keep four lines of text under the picture; HGR2 is the whole screen, so there you type TEXT blind, as you did. The text page lives at 1024, so POKE 1024,193 puts an A in the corner and POKE -16302,0 flips a picture to full screen. The rest of zero page is real too: POKE 34,20 for a status line, POKE 50,63 for inverse, PEEK(-16336) for the speaker.

DOS 3.3

Files work the way they did: PRINT CHR$(4);"OPEN SCORES", then WRITE, READ, CLOSE, and CATALOG typed at the prompt. They live in the emulator's disk for this visit.
